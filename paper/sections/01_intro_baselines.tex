\section{Introduction and Empirical SOTA Baselines}
\label{sec:intro}

Lossless image compression maps a finite discrete array $X \in \Z_M^{H \times W \times C}$ ($M = 2^B$, typically $B \in \{8,12,14,16\}$ and $C \in \{1,3,4\}$) bijectively onto a prefix-free binary string $\mathcal{B}(X) \in \{0,1\}^*$ minimizing expected codelength $\E[|\mathcal{B}(X)|]$. Across the literature, two rate conventions coexist for $C$-channel images:
\begin{equation}
  \mathrm{bpsp} \triangleq \frac{|\mathcal{B}(X)|}{H \cdot W \cdot C} \quad \text{(bits per sub-pixel)}, \qquad
  \mathrm{bpp} \triangleq \frac{|\mathcal{B}(X)|}{H \cdot W} = C \cdot \mathrm{bpsp} \quad \text{(bits per pixel)},
\end{equation}
so uncompressed 8-bit RGB24 corresponds to $8.00\bpsp = 24.00\bpp$.

Over three decades of classical engineering---from scanline predictors (\texttt{PNG}), Median Edge Detection in \texttt{JPEG-LS}~\citep{weinberger2000jpegls}, Gradient-Adjusted Prediction in \texttt{CALIC}~\citep{wu1997calic}, reversible LeGall 5/3 wavelets in \texttt{JPEG~2000}~\citep{taubman2002jpeg2000}, and 2D backward references in \texttt{WebP-L}/\texttt{AVIF}, to \texttt{MANIAC} decision trees in \texttt{FLIF}~\citep{sneyers2016flif} and \texttt{Squeeze}+\texttt{MA}+\texttt{rANS} in \texttt{JPEG~XL}~\citep{alakuijala2019jpegxl,duda2013rans}---classical rates on Kodak have converged near $2.87\bpsp$. Conversely, neural codecs---hierarchical priors (\texttt{L3C}~\citep{mentzer2019l3c}, \texttt{SReC}~\citep{cao2020srec}), deep integer lifting (\texttt{iWave++}~\citep{ma2020iwave}), discrete flows and bits-back coders (\texttt{IDF}~\citep{hoogeboom2019idf}, \texttt{iFlow}~\citep{zhang2021iflow}, \texttt{BB-ANS}~\citep{townsend2019bbans}, \texttt{Bit-Swap}~\citep{kingma2019bitswap}), lossy-plus-residual/bit-plane autoregression (\texttt{DLPR}~\citep{bai2024dlpr}, \texttt{ArIB-BPS}~\citep{zhang2024arib}), and test-time SGD fine-tuned ConvNets (\texttt{CALLIC}~\citep{callic2025}, \texttt{HPAC}/\texttt{HPAC-FT}~\citep{hpac2025})---reach $2.52\bpsp$ only via multi-second per-image GPU backpropagation and non-deterministic floating-point arithmetic (\Cref{tab:sota-baselines}).

The central thesis of \texttt{vpxc} is that beating both \texttt{JPEG~XL} and \texttt{HPAC-FT} without test-time SGD requires attacking the \emph{exact information-theoretic mechanisms} where current codecs pay structural KL divergence: non-stationary contour bracketing, camera ISP/CFA submanifolds, sensor shot-noise fusion, integer rounding total correlation, and deterministic fixed-point long-range state-space context.
