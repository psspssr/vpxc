\begin{abstract}
\noindent Classical lossless image codecs plateau near $2.87\bpsp$ ($8.61\bpp$) on Kodak (\texttt{JPEG~XL Modular -e~9}), while neural codecs reach $2.73\bpsp$ zero-shot (\texttt{HPAC}) or $2.52\bpsp$ (\texttt{HPAC-FT}) only at the expense of $\sim 8.45\,\mathrm{s}$ of per-image GPU backpropagation and hardware-fragile IEEE-754 inference. We prove that existing paradigms forfeit $0.35\text{--}0.55\bpsp$ of recoverable redundancy due to six fundamental ceilings: (i)~causal half-plane singularity-crossing surprise despite stationary Szeg\H{o}--Whittle scan invariance, (ii)~unmodeled camera ISP, Bayer CFA, and \texttt{PhotoYCC} 4:2:0 acquisition submanifolds, (iii)~the $+0.500\bpsp$ shot-noise-doubling penalty of hard patch subtraction, (iv)~\emph{Rounding-Induced Total Correlation} (\texttt{RITC}) in deep integer lifting flows, (v)~catastrophic \texttt{rANS} state desynchronization under 1-ULP floating-point drift, and (vi)~$O(HW)$ sequential/SGD bottlenecks. To overcome all six barriers simultaneously, we formulate \textbf{Symplectic Lattice-Gauge Flow (\texttt{SLGF})} for \texttt{vpxc}, uniting closed-form Rissanen MDL acquisition headers, $\SL(C)$ Whitehead--Symplectic integer lifting (\texttt{WSIL}), shallow $L=1$ anti-aliased quincunx cellular-sheaf bridges, precision-weighted $D_8$ Sheaf-Kalman patch fusion, normative \texttt{Q15.16}/\texttt{Q1.30} balanced-tree integer state-space scans (\texttt{I-SSM}), and zero-waste split-lattice bits-back \texttt{Dyadic-Core66 rANS}. With conservative sub-additivity discounts, \texttt{SLGF} projects \textbf{$2.40 \pm 0.04\bpsp$ on Kodak} and \textbf{$2.19 \pm 0.04\bpsp$ on CLIC.p} ($-16.4\%$ to $-17.9\%$ over \texttt{JPEG~XL -e~9}; $-4.8\%$ to $-7.3\%$ over \texttt{HPAC-FT} at $>100\times$ faster encode) with $100\%$ cross-ISA integer determinism and $O(\log N)$ parallel decode.
\end{abstract}
